## ✦ Creative Direction & Visual Identity

- **Aesthetic**: Monochrome Anime & Minimal Japanese Editorial Design.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Main Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary Background: `#F7F7F7`
  - Highlight Background: `#EFEFEF`
  - Primary Text: `#111111`
  - Secondary Text: `#555555`
  - Borders: `#E5E5E5` / `#111111`
  - Accent: `#222222`
- **Character System**: **100% original black-and-white manga vector illustrations** with fine ink outlines and subtle grayscale shading.
  - **Stuti's Digital Mascot**: Expressive anime character with distinctive manga hairstyle, stylish outfit with collar & ID badge, and dynamic reactive poses (`welcome`, `waving`, `laptop`, `coding`, `thinking`, `researching`, `observing`, `celebrating`).
  - **Doodle Creatures**: Cute white ghost (`GhostMascot`), tiny bunny (`BunnyMascot`), floating star character (`StarCreature`), and little research robot (`MiniRobotMascot`).
- **Typography Matrix**:
  - **Headings**: *Space Grotesk*
  - **Body Text**: *Inter*
  - **Terminal / Labels**: *JetBrains Mono*
  - **Handwritten Notes**: *Kalam*
- **Subtle 3D Scene**: Three.js & React Three Fiber matte off-white sphere with delicate black wireframe orbital rings and floating geometric icosahedron crystals.
